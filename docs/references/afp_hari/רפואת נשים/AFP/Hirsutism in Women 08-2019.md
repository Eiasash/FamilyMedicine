## Abstract

The Ferriman-Gallwey score for hirsutism. A score of 1 to 4 is given for nine areas of the body. A total score less than
 8 is considered normal;a score of 8 to 15 indicates mild hirsutism;and a score greater than 15 indicates moderate to
 severe hirsutism. A score of 0 indicates absence of terminal hair.
