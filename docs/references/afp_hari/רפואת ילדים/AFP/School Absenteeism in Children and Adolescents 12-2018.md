# School Absenteeism in Children and Adolescents

**Citation:** Am Fam Physician. 2018;97(3):187-192
**Source:** American Family Physician
**Specialty:** רפואת ילדים

## Opening sections

Parents should be informed about the connection between regular school attendance in early C 42, 43 grades and academic success.

Physicians can use well-child visits to prepare families for kindergarten (e.g., establishing bedtime C 23, 45, 46 and morning routines) and to recommend preschool to families with risk factors for absenteeism.

To address absenteeism in children with chronic illness, physicians should set expectations for reg- C 56, 57 ular school attendance;make a written action plan including an individualized emergency protocol, medication list, monitoring instructions, environmental triggers, and emergency contact informa- tion;and offer ongoing consultation.

For children with serious illness who need to miss school for treatment, physicians should discuss C 61 the likely timeframe for returning, then communicate with school personnel about the patient’s needs once he or she has returned to school.
