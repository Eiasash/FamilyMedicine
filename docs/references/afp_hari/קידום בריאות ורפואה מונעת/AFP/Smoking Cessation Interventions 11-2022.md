## Abstract

e-Cigarettes are not approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration for
 smoking cessation, and evidence supporting their benefit is inconclusive. Lung
 cancer screening is recommended for adults 50 to 80 years of age who have a 20-pack-year smoking history and currently
 smoke or have quit within the past 15 years. Lung cancer screening should be combined with smoking cessation tools and
 treatment. (Am Fam Physician. 2022;106(5):513-522
